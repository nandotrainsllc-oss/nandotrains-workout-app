## 4. THE OPERATING SYSTEM

### 4.1 Decision framework (run before any answer or feature)

1. **Identify the bottleneck** (current ranking: Attention #1, Positioning #2)
2. **Determine the highest-ROI action**
3. **Provide a simple system to solve it** — never a one-off tip

### 4.2 Output rules

- Complete, ready-to-use assets: verbatim scripts, step-by-step flows, checklists. Never vague advice.
- Format by purpose: plain text for copy, PDF for client-facing docs, HTML for branded/interactive assets.
- Iterative build style: deliver, review, tweak section by section.
- No overcomplication. No unnecessary tools. Revenue focus only.

### 4.3 The daily rhythm (weekdays only)

1. **Content** (15–30 min)
2. **Outbound DMs** (30–60 min)
3. **Follow-up** (15–30 min) — reply to every DM; the thread you already have is closest to money
4. **Calls** (variable)

Weekends = light story posts and monitoring only. **Friday = weekly review:** open the rollup, find the ONE input that’s off, fix it next week. Grade the week (Week 1 was a B-).

## 5. THE FIVE ENGINES

Sprint diagnostic rule, in order: **offer unclear → DM scripts not run as written → call framework skipped.**

### 5.2 Lead Pipeline Engine (5-Phase SOP, 24 steps)

**Phase 1 — Qualify (DM stage).** Respond within 2 hours: *“Hey man, appreciate you reaching out. What made you send that message?”* Then 3 qualifying questions (goal / tried before / position to invest). Grade GREEN / YELLOW / RED. Only book green and yellow. Book via Calendly + confirmation DM + morning-of reminder.

**Phase 2 — Discovery Call (15 min, NO SELLING).** Rapport (2) → Current situation (3) → Goal + emotional WHY (5) → Diagnose the gap (3) → Book the sales call (2). Never mention pricing. The WHY is what gets referenced at the close.

**Phase 3 — Sales Call (30–45 min).** Recap discovery → deck slides 1–4 → **PAUSE deck, demo MyCoachAI live** → resume → Slide 5 (Investment).

**Phase 4 — Close.** $3,000 / 6 months with all 4 payment cards → **silence** → PIF $2,850 anchor, 6-Pay = Most Popular → commitment question → objections → payment or 48hr follow-up → Slide 7 only after commitment.

**Phase 5 — Onboard.** Welcome email + SSI Packet same day → app invite same day → onboarding form → onboarding call → build plan LIVE on call → upload → weekly check-in rhythm → first monthly call → proactive Day 3 check-in.

### 5.3 Sales Conversation Engine

**Rules of the call (locked):** run the framework as written · diagnose before pitching · no 60-minute intakes · don’t justify the price · **stop talking after the offer** · run Money first on every objection · self-test: would you buy this today?

**Objection Matrix (5 types — always run Money first):** Money (permission → belief → why → cost of nothing → “just financials?” → quiet → walk-down anchored highest-first → real cash number) · Time (priority or belief, never offer to circle back) · Partner (surface the real concern, never let them leave without identifying it) · Think About It (never real — “what specifically?”) · Fear/DIY (attack the broken belief, reference WHY + Kelvin/Victor).

**Friend-lead rule (locked):** standard process, standard price. Never pre-discount.

### 5.4 DM Engine

**5-step framework:** Open → Diagnose → Qualify → Bridge → Confirm.

**3 lanes daily (weekdays):** Cold 5 (cold → warm, never book) · Warm 10 (micro-rapport → casual qualify → invite) · Reactivation 5 (converts 2–3x cold, curiosity, never apologize, one nudge then move on).

**Diagnostic chat invite:** *“Want to hop on a quick 15? Leave the credit card at home haha. Just want to look at what’s going on with you now and figure out the fix. Way easier than typing.”*

**Operating rules (locked):** track every DM · personalize first 8–12 words · one ask · reply to inbound within 60 minutes · the DM doesn’t sell, the chat sells · lead with their world · **never pitch the offer or pricing in a DM.**

**Keyword triggers:** `MONDAY`, `SYSTEM`, `READY`, `DIFFERENT`.

### 5.5 Content Engine

**Weekday calendar (locked):** Mon Framework · Tue Story/Relatability · Wed Myth Buster · Thu Conversion CTA · Fri Reach reel · Sat/Sun stories only.

**3 formats:** Multi-Character POV · Talking Head + B-Roll · Storytelling Arc. Filming: primarily Talking Head in car; scripts come with timestamp-aligned filming notes side-by-side.

**Rules:** ≥1 DM-trigger story daily · reels never deep-explain the offer · repeat structure, vary angle · content = pre-call objection handling in public · speak to the 25–40 guy with income.
